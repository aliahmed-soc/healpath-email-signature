How to add it in Zoho Mail

  1. Fill in your name, job title, email and phone above. Check the preview looks right.
  2. Press Copy signature, the button next to the word Preview.
  3. Open mail.zoho.com. Click the gear icon at the top right, then Settings.
  4. In the left menu click Mail, then Signature. Click New Signature.
  5. In Signature Name type HealPath. This box cannot be left empty.
  6. Click inside the big empty box below it. Press Ctrl + V. You should see the blue HealPath text and your name in blue.
  7. For Signature position choose Place signature above quoted content.
  8. In both Associated From addresses boxes, type your HealPath email address. Keep the tick on Add signature to all my email replies and forwards.
  9. Click Save. Send a test email to yourself and open it on your phone as well.
Already have an old signature? Open it, click in the box, press Ctrl + A then Delete so it is fully empty, and only then paste the new one. Old text left behind will keep showing on every email.
